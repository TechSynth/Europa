Jimenez Diaz

Jimenez is based between Barcelona and Berlin. Her work on the FKA twigs videos got a lot of attention, but she doesn't talk about her process much. She tends to lock herself in a room with the track for three days before drawing up a single storyboard. I always notice how she uses silence in her commercial work. It's disarming.
