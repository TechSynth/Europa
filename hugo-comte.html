Hugo Comte

Hugo's photography looks like a still from a 90s action movie you can't quite remember. The colors are heavy, the contrast is aggressive, and nobody in the frame looks like they're having fun. It works perfectly for high fashion. He usually shoots from low angles to make the subjects look terrifying.

His cover for Dua Lipa's Future Nostalgia is probably everywhere by now, but what I keep going back to is his work for Heaven by Marc Jacobs. It feels claustrophobic in the best way.
